# Governance

Metricfixer maintains this repository and controls official releases. Changes to the frozen publication dataset require a new release and a documented rationale. Maintainers may accept community reruns and extensions, but third-party results are not treated as official Metricfixer benchmark data unless explicitly reviewed and released by Metricfixer.

The repository distinguishes three layers:

1. immutable published data;
2. maintained runner and verification tooling;
3. proposed or community experiments.

No layer may silently replace another.
